# Satyfinance

Satyfinance is a personal finance dashboard for tracking spending, budgets, upcoming bills, and the ongoing cost of long-lasting purchases.

## Features

- **Dashboard:** Monthly spending, budget progress, scheduled bills, and daily averages.
- **Quick add:** Record expenses in plain language; Satyfinance detects the amount, date, and category.
- **Product lifespan:** Track a purchase over its useful life and see its daily and monthly cost.
- **Calendar:** View expenses and scheduled payments by date.
- **Transactions and analytics:** Search and filter expenses, then review category and spending summaries.
- **Data backups:** Export records as JSON or CSV, or restore them from a JSON backup.
- **Mobile-friendly:** Use the dashboard and navigation on smaller screens.

## Privacy

Financial records are stored in your browser. No account is required.

## Deployment

Satyfinance is deployed on Vercel.
